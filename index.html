Chicken Curry & Rice
মুরগির তরকারি
30
min
Easy
difficulty
৳180
cost

Ingredients

4servings
Chicken500g
Onion200g
Tomato150g
Garlic4 cloves
Ginger1 inch
Turmeric1 tsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Oil3 tbsp
Rice400g

Steps

1
Marinate chicken with turmeric, chilli, salt and yogurt. 15 min.
2
Heat oil. Fry onions until golden. 5 min.
3
Add garlic-ginger paste. 1 min.
4
Add tomatoes. Cook until oil separates.
5
Add chicken. Sear on high 5 min.
6
Add water. Simmer 20 min covered.
7
Serve with steamed rice and lime.
